Mji's Screenshot Combo AI

Capture screenshots → let local Ollama read them → correct and approve → keep text and remove the screenshot.

1. Capture screenshots

Cached guide videos are reused. Otherwise the tool downloads the guide; if blocked, provide your local video. For your own screenshots, name each image with its source time in seconds, for example 74.5.png. Unnamed times must be corrected during review. Captures are sampled; check the source for missed combos.

2. Read with Ollama

Connecting…

For captured files, click “Read / resume with Ollama”. A browser recording starts screenshot capture and reading automatically after you stop recording. Keep this script's console open while it works. Completed readings are cached. Your approved corrections are reused as text examples; the model's weights are not retrained.

3. Review a screenshot

Watch source timestamp ↗

Approval permanently removes only the tool's screenshot copy after saving the reviewed text. Imported original images and source videos stay in their original folders. Add only corrections you have checked; incorrect approved examples can reduce future accuracy.

4. Update your app

Only approved text records are exported. Screenshot images are never published. Check for duplicate sampled routes before approving them.
